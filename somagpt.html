SomaGPT: exploring AI for learning.

SomaGPT is an education technology project exploring how AI can support learners as they study, find explanations and navigate educational information. This portfolio page explains the project’s direction; visit the live project to review its current experience and available features.

Why AI and education?

Learning often involves moving between notes, questions, explanations and practice. AI-powered tools can potentially make that process more conversational by helping a learner explore a topic or formulate a follow-up question. They also have limitations: generated answers can be incomplete or incorrect, and important academic, medical, legal or other high-stakes information should be checked against reliable sources.

The product direction

SomaGPT’s stated focus is practical learning support, personalized study exploration and educational guidance. The useful test for any feature is whether it helps a learner make progress without hiding uncertainty or replacing their own understanding.

  • Clarity: explanations should be understandable and relevant to the learner’s question.
  • Learning support: encourage follow-up questions, revision and active understanding rather than only giving an answer.
  • Responsible use: make the limits of AI-generated information clear and encourage checking important claims.
  • Access: a web-based experience can be reached across supported devices without requiring a traditional desktop setup.
