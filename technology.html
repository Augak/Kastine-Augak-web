TECHNOLOGY & PRODUCT DESIGN

Practical technology, designed around people.

My interests sit across software development, applied AI and digital product design. I’m drawn to projects that turn a clear problem into a reliable, understandable tool — especially on mobile devices and in contexts where data, device capacity or connectivity may be limited.

Web development

Building responsive websites with clear structure, semantic HTML, accessible controls and useful content. Strong foundations help visitors and search engines understand what a page is for.

Mobile-first apps

Progressive web app patterns can make tools installable and comfortable to use on Android, while keeping interfaces lightweight and navigation familiar.

Applied AI

AI is most useful when its role is specific: supporting a learning task, helping organize information or assisting a research workflow. Important claims should remain open to verification.

Education technology

Learning products should help people find explanations, practice skills and navigate material without making unsupported promises about outcomes.

Accessibility & usability

Readable contrast, keyboard support, screen-reader labels, reduced-motion options and clear feedback make products more usable for more people.

SEO & GEO

Helpful page copy, descriptive titles, internal links, accurate structured data and crawlable sitemaps make a site easier for people, search engines and answer engines to interpret.

A PRACTICAL BUILDING PRINCIPLE

Real functionality over impressive-looking demos.

A polished interface is only the beginning. A dependable product needs clear states, useful error messages, secure handling of secrets, sensible performance and honest communication about what is implemented. Where a feature depends on an external API or service, it should be tested against that service rather than represented by invented results.

For projects intended for Uganda and East Africa, I also value affordability, Android usability and designs that work well on everyday devices. These considerations are part of product quality, not optional extras.

Projects that put these ideas into practice

SomaGPT explores AI-assisted learning. iVerifyAI explores claim verification and evidence-led assessment of online information. Stemit PWA explores mobile-first audio workflows. These projects represent different experiments, and their current feature status should be checked on each project or repository.
