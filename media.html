ABOUT TECH

A technology-focused podcast identity for conversations about useful digital tools, software, AI, online safety, creative technology and the people building with it. The aim is to make technical topics approachable without losing the important details.

Potential formats include explainers, product walkthroughs, thoughtful interviews and comparisons of how digital tools work in practice.
