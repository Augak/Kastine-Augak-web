A PRACTICAL POINT OF VIEW

Technology should feel useful, not complicated.

I care about the moment an idea moves beyond a screen and starts helping someone do something. That might be a website that explains a project clearly, a small Android-friendly tool, an experiment in AI-supported learning or a piece of media that makes a difficult subject easier to understand.

My approach is straightforward: listen to the need, make the experience clear, test what matters and keep improving. Good design can be expressive without getting in the way.

LOCAL CONTEXT, OPEN POSSIBILITIES

Built with everyday realities in mind.

For people building and learning in Uganda, technology has to meet the reality of the device in their hand, the connection available and the time they can give it. I value products that stay clear, load sensibly and respect people's attention.

The ambition is not to make everything look futuristic. It is to make the right thing easier to use.
